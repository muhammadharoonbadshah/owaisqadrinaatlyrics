Welcome

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Ubaidi Naat Lyrics Collection application collects, uses and protects information while you use the app.

Information We Collect

The application does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Home Address
  • Contacts
  • Precise Device Location

The app is designed so that users can browse, search, stream and read Naat lyrics without providing personal information.

Permissions Used

🎤 Microphone

The microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is used only for the Voice Search feature. Your speech is converted into text for searching Naat lyrics.

Voice Search:
  • No audio is recorded.
  • No audio is stored.
  • No audio is shared.

🌐 Internet

Internet access is required to:

  • Load updated lyrics.
  • Retrieve database updates from Firebase.
  • Play YouTube videos.
  • Stream MP3 Naats.
  • Download MP3 Naats selected by the user.

🔔 Notifications

Notifications are used to display download progress and media playback controls.

📁 Storage

Downloaded MP3 Naats are stored locally on your device for offline listening. The application does not upload your downloaded files to our servers.

Firebase Services

The application uses Google Firebase services to provide:

  • Database updates
  • Application resources
  • Cloud storage for application content

Firebase may automatically collect technical information such as device type, application version, crash diagnostics (if enabled), IP address and usage information according to Google's Privacy Policy.
