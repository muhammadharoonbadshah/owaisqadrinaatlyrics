Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Welcome

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Owais Qadri Naat Lyrics application (formerly "Ubaidi Naat Lyrics", same app, package com.ubaidi.naatlyrics.app) collects, uses, and protects information while you use the app, and how advertising is shown within it.

Information We Collect

The application does not require account registration and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Precise device location

The app is designed so users can browse, search, stream, and read Naat lyrics without providing personal information.

Advertising & Google AdMob

This app is monetized using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC that displays advertisements within the app. AdMob and its partner ad networks may collect and use certain information to serve ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
  • Device type, operating system, and general technical information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not precise GPS)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads (e.g. impressions, clicks)

Personalized vs. non-personalized ads:

Depending on your region, consent settings, and device preferences, you may see personalized ads (based on interests inferred from your advertising ID) or non-personalized ads (based only on contextual information, not your ad history). Where required by law (such as under GDPR or similar regulations), a consent prompt will ask for your preference before personalized ads are shown.

Managing ad personalization:

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time from your device settings:
Android: Settings > Google > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalization" / "Delete advertising ID".

AdMob's data handling is governed by Google's own privacy and advertising policies, not by us directly:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

How Google uses information from partner sites or apps:

Permissions Used

Microphone

The microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is used only for the Voice Search feature. Your speech is converted into text locally for searching Naat lyrics.

Voice Search:
  • No audio is recorded to a file.
  • No audio is stored on our servers.
  • No audio is shared with third parties.

Internet

Internet access is required to:

  • Load updated lyrics
  • Retrieve database updates from Firebase
  • Stream and download MP3 Naats
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob

Notifications & Foreground Service

Notifications and the media playback foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) are used to continue audio playback and show playback controls when the app is minimized or the screen is locked.

Storage

Downloaded MP3 Naats are stored locally on your device for offline listening. The application does not upload your downloaded files to our servers.

Firebase Services

The application uses Google Firebase services to provide:

  • Database updates
  • Application resources
  • Cloud storage for application content

Firebase may automatically collect technical information such as device type, app version, crash diagnostics (if enabled), IP address, and usage information according to Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Privacy Policy:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Downloaded MP3 Naats

Users may download MP3 Naats for offline listening. Downloaded files remain on the user's device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information is shared only when required by law, or through the normal operation of the third-party services described above (Firebase, Google AdMob).

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect application data. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

The application is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising shown through Google AdMob is configured to follow Google's policies on child-directed treatment where applicable.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page together with a revised effective date.
